# Hi there 👋

[lychee.is](https://lychee.is) is a SaaS platform built for high-touch retail teams.

We provide tools that help retailers run day-to-day operations while keeping customer relationships at the center of the business.

### What Lychee helps with

- Daily retail operations and team workflows
- Store-level execution and coordination
- Customer lifecycle management
- A highly configurable CRM tailored to each business

Our goal is to give retail teams the flexibility to operate efficiently and deliver a better customer experience.
